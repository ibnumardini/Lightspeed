Privacy Policy: Lightspeed - Video Speed Controller Pro

Lightspeed - Video Speed Controller Pro ("Lightspeed") respects your privacy.

Data Collection

Lightspeed does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, browsing history, analytics, or telemetry.

Local Storage

All settings (playback speed preferences, quick speed presets, keyboard shortcuts, layout toggles, and accent color) are stored locally in your browser using the browser's extension storage API (storage.local / storage.session). This data never leaves your device.

Permissions Used

  • storage Saves your extension settings and per-site speed preferences locally.
  • tabs Reads the active tab's hostname to apply per-tab, per-site, or all-tabs playback speed settings and updates the toolbar badge.
  • <all_urls> Detects <video> elements and listens for your configured keyboard shortcuts on websites you visit. No page content is collected or transmitted.

Third-Party Links

Lightspeed includes optional external links (such as Trakteer, Ko-fi, or the developer's website). Clicking these links is entirely optional and subject to those third-party sites' privacy policies.
